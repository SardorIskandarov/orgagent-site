OrgAgent

A private work assistant that runs on your own computer.

OrgAgent reads the Gmail labels, calendar and Telegram chats you choose, shows what matters first, finds tasks and deadlines, answers questions about your messages, and drafts replies. A reply is sent only when you review it and press Send.

It is installed by organisations for their staff. Each person's messages stay on their own computer; the AI model runs on the organisation's own server.

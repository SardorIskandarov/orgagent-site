Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

OrgAgent is software installed on a person's own computer by their organisation. There is no OrgAgent cloud service: your data is not sent to the developer of OrgAgent.

What OrgAgent accesses in your Google account

  • Gmail, read-only: the emails under the labels you choose (sender, subject, date, text and supported attachments), to show and sort them, find tasks and deadlines, answer your questions and draft replies.
  • Sending email, only if you turn it on: OrgAgent sends a reply only after you have reviewed it and pressed Send, and only to the recipient you saw.
  • Google Calendar, read-only: your events, to show your day and warn about conflicts.
  • Your email address: to show which account is connected.

Where it is kept

On your computer only, in OrgAgent's folder in your user profile. Your Google sign-in is stored encrypted. OrgAgent keeps a copy of the selected messages so it can work without asking Google every time.

Who it is shared with

  • Your organisation's AI server. To read and sort messages, OrgAgent sends their text to the AI model on your organisation's own server. The server returns the answer and does not store message text; it keeps only counts (number of requests, which computer, which OrgAgent version) so the administrator can keep it running fairly.
  • Google, when OrgAgent reads your data or sends an email you approved.
  • No one else. Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models.

OrgAgent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long it is kept, and your choices

  • Removing a label or chat from OrgAgent deletes its stored messages at once.
  • Disconnecting Gmail in OrgAgent (Settings → Connections) removes OrgAgent's access at Google and deletes the stored emails. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling OrgAgent (Windows Settings → Apps) can delete all of its data from your computer.

Contact

Questions about OrgAgent at your organisation: contact your organisation's OrgAgent administrator.
